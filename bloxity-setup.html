Short Description

Description
Your car has a jet under the seat. Drive along sky-high roads over the city, then hold FLY to jet across the gaps, up through the windows in great painted frames, through rings for Coins and across the rooftops of the towers, all on one tank of gas that fills again on the road. 100 stages through four worlds, harder all the way: Sunburst City, Mesa Motorway, Neon Nightway and Glacier Pass, with ramps, big air, laser gates, crumbling roads, trucks to jet over and icy bends. Every few minutes a Police Chase: race the other players to the Safehouse while the cops close in behind you; whoever they catch is BUSTED. Earn Coins, Gems and Keys to buy 7 cars, tune-ups, 16 paints and crates of jet exhausts. Easy, Normal and Hard modes, daily tasks, quests, FREE gifts, a daily reward, codes and leaderboards. Play on a phone (upright or sideways), a tablet or a computer.

Hosting steps

  1. GitHub: create the empty repo LEGiON-Platforms/jetcar-getaway (no README) and add the repo secret LEGION_DEPLOY_TOKEN.
  2. hosting.bloxity.io: the app jetcar-getaway must exist (its id is GAME_ID in .github/workflows/deploy.yml).
  3. Push the code to main (as asivixlp44-cmyk): the workflow builds the server image and the client and deploys both.
  4. Check https://jetcar-getaway.host.bloxity.io/health (it should say "db": true: Legion provides MONGODB_URI) and play at https://jetcar-getaway.play.bloxity.io.
  5. Create the game in Manage Games with the values above (slug exactly jetcar-getaway).
